# Figures

> Figures is a European compensation management and pay transparency platform that helps HR teams and managers make strategic salary decisions using real-time market data. It combines salary benchmarking across 3.5M+ datapoints, dynamic salary bands, collaborative compensation review workflows, and pay equity analysis, with modules built for the EU Pay Transparency Directive. Figures connects to 30+ HRIS and performance-review systems and is backed by Point Nine and Seedcamp. Figures does not publish a public developer API; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog profile.
